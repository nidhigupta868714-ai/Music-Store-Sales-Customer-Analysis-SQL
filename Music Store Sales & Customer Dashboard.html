💡 Insights (update with filters)

    🎯 Business recommendations

    • Promote the Rock catalogue first. Rock drives the majority of revenue, and every customer has bought it, so it is the safest base for campaigns and bundles.
    • Prague is the festival city. It has the highest city-level sales, which answers the project's "where to host the music festival" question.
    • Grow beyond the USA. USA and Canada lead, so Brazil, France and Germany are the next markets for targeted offers.
    • Reward the top 10 customers. A small group of loyal buyers contributes a visible share of revenue, so loyalty offers are worth testing.
    • Revenue is flat year over year. Growth needs new levers such as bundles, upsells or new markets rather than only more of the same.
